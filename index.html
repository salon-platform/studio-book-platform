The part nobody advertises

We never take a cut of your bookings.

Most salon software makes its real money on the way through — a slice of every card you run, and sometimes a commission on clients who found you through their app. Here is what that looks like across the tools salons actually use, as published by each company on 2026-08-01.

ToolMonthlyCut of your money
Booksy$29.99 + $20/stylist 2.49–2.69% of card payments, plus 30% of a new marketplace client's first visit (min $10, capped at $100) if Boost is on
Fresha$19.95 solo 2.29–3.30% of card payments, plus 20% of a new marketplace client's first visit (min $6), plus metered texts
GlossGenius$28–$1682.6% of every card payment
Vagaro$23.99 + $10/calendar2.29–3.5% of card payments, plus network and chargeback fees
Square$0–$1492.4–3.3% of card payments
Salon Vine$19–$59 Nothing. Zero percent, forever.

Here is how that actually works, because “0%” deserves an explanation: your money never passes through us. If you turn on deposits, your client pays through your own Stripe account and it lands in your bank — we never hold it, never route it, and never take a cent of it. That is why zero percent is a promise we can keep rather than a launch offer.

What we don't have yet is full checkout — card-on-file for the whole service, tips at the chair, automatic no-show charges beyond the deposit. If that is what your day depends on, one of the tools above serves you better today and we would rather say so than take your $19. If you want a booking page that looks like your salon, holds a deposit so no-shows cost them instead of you, and never taxes your revenue, that is exactly what we built.

Questions

Fair questions, straight answers

If yours isn't here, start a site and reply to the welcome email — we answer those personally.

What does it actually cost?

Your first 30 days are free. We ask for a card at signup to hold your plan, and nothing is charged until day 31 — cancel before then and you are never charged a cent. After that it is $19, $39 or $59 a month depending on how many stylists you need, with no setup fee and no contract. Cancelling takes two clicks in your portal; you never have to call anyone or send an email to get out.

Do I need tech skills?

If you can fill out four short steps on your phone, you're qualified. Type your salon's name, tap a theme, add photos and services if you have them handy, done. There's nothing to install, host, or update — ever.

How fast is my site live?

The moment you finish the wizard your site is on the web at salonvine.com/s/your-salon. Across the salons built so far, average setup time is under 2 minutes — most of that is picking your favorite photos.

Can clients book online?

Clients send a booking request through your site — their name, contact info, and what they want — and it lands in your email instantly. You reply to confirm the time, so you keep full control of your book. It's not a real-time calendar yet; a full booking calendar is on our roadmap for the Elite plan, and we won't advertise it until it ships.

What if I already have a website or use Booksy?

Keep them — nothing conflicts. Your Salon Vine site works alongside anything: many salons use it as the clean "link in bio" page that shows off their work and catches booking requests, while their old tools keep doing their thing. If you decide to switch over completely, paste your current site's address into the signup and we'll help move things across.

Do you take a percentage of my bookings?

No — zero, and that will not change. Some booking apps charge a commission on clients who find you through their marketplace: Booksy takes 30% of that client's first visit and Fresha takes 20%. We have no marketplace and we never touch your money, so there is nothing for us to take a percentage of. Deposits are the one place a card is involved, and even there the money moves through your own Stripe account straight to your bank — we never touch it, so there is still nothing for us to take a percentage of.

What happens on day 31?

Your plan starts billing at the price you picked, on the card you entered at signup, and it renews monthly until you stop it. We are not going to surprise you with a different plan than the one you chose. If you cancel before day 31 you are never charged, and your site stays up through the end of the trial either way.

What don't you have yet?

Worth knowing before you spend two minutes: there is no real-time booking calendar, no full checkout or card-on-file, no SMS reminders, and no phone app — Salon Vine is a web app you can pin to your home screen. Clients send booking requests and you confirm them. Deposits are live on Studio Pro, through your own Stripe account. We would rather you hear all of that from us now than feel misled in week two.
